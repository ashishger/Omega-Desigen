About Omega Design

Designing homes that are beautiful on paper and practical in real life.

Omega Design is a residential planning and design consultancy based in Tish, Beagumpur, Hooghly, West Bengal. Since 2021, we have helped families and builders create modern houses with clear planning, detailed visualization, and execution-aware guidance.

We believe good design should feel premium, solve day-to-day lifestyle needs, and remain practical for local construction methods and budgets.

Studio Snapshot

  • Focused on residential projects and family homes
  • Experienced in 2D planning, 3D visuals, and elevation concepts
  • Direct collaboration with clients from first call to final drawing
  • Support with design presentation for approval workflows

Our Approach

Every project begins with your lifestyle, not just room count.

Before drawing starts, we gather details about who will use each space, how your daily routine flows, and which design style feels right for your family. This allows us to create plans that are efficient, comfortable, and future-ready.

From staircase placement and ventilation logic to facade proportions and interior mood direction, we refine decisions step by step so you can move to construction with confidence.
