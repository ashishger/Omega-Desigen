About Omega Design

A planning studio focused on comfort, ventilation, flow, and buildability.

Based in Tish, Beagumpur, Hooghly, Omega Design works with clients who want design clarity before construction begins. We study your plot, priorities, and budget to shape options that are practical and elegant.

Our approach balances modern visual style with real life requirements like family movement, natural light, storage logic, staircase comfort, and future expansion possibilities.
